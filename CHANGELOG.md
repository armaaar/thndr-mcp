## 0.1.0 (2026-10-06)

First public version: an unofficial MCP server and CLI for Thndr covering the Egypt, US, UAE and simulator markets —
market data, account, orders, activity, returns and trading journal, watchlists and price alerts, portfolio analytics,
financials, news, dividends, movers, tags and economic data — read-only with respect to money.
